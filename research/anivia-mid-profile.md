# Anivia mid: provisional strategic profile

Research date: 2026-09-03. Status: provisional; no human review recorded.
The profile describes a real champion, not fictional test data. The color
classification is an AI-assisted coaching interpretation, not a Riot label.

## Evidence and provenance

### Mechanics

[Riot's Anivia page](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/anivia/)
describes Flash Frost as an area-damage projectile with a stun on detonation.
[Riot's legacy champion page](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champion/anivia/)
also describes the movement-blocking wall. The latter was available through a
search excerpt and is an older source, not a current patch audit.

The [Wiki ability template](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template%3AData_Anivia/Glacial_Storm)
was available as an indexed excerpt: it describes recurring area damage, slowing,
mana upkeep and termination conditions. Direct access to the main Wiki article
was blocked. The indexed template is old; no numeric ranges, costs or current
patch accuracy are asserted here.

### Observed role context, not color evidence

[LoLalytics Anivia mid](https://lolalytics.com/lol/anivia/build/) showed:

- provider patch label: 16.17;
- region: Global; rank bracket: Emerald+; queue: Ranked Solo/Duo;
- mid sample: 35,095 games at retrieval.

This is a dated observation of a changing page, not an imported live dataset.
The sample supports considering mid as an observed role, not as her exclusive
role. No build preference, flex-role probability, matchup edge or color is
inferred from this sample. No win-rate estimate is used by the profile.

## DraftOS interpretation

- Main Blue: controlling space and denying routes or actions is a plausible
  common thread across stun, wall and persistent area pressure.
- Off White: defensive zoning and peel can support an allied plan. This is a
  conditional interpretation, not proof of broad flexibility or universal safety.
- No additional colors are assigned without a specific supporting argument.

## Conditions and counter-hypotheses

Wave clear is a capability, not guaranteed access to a wave. Long-range pressure
may prevent safe setup; this is a hypothesis to test, not a measured Xerath
matchup result. Mana availability, allied protection, approach routes and wall
placement can change whether the intended plan is executable.

The profile's patch remains null: a provider's statistics patch alone does not
establish that the whole strategic assessment was verified for that patch.
Before marking reviewed, verify current mechanics and builds, specify the target
patch, and obtain domain review of the White conditions and Blue limitations.
No calibrated win probability or causal advantage is claimed.
